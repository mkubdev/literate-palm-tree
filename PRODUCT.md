# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, Tailwind CSS v4, Biome, pnpm — same base as `nextjs-fleezy`. Sanity CMS planned so Simon can edit prices, hours and photos himself (wired after the art direction is chosen). French only, no i18n.

## Users

People living or working in Lyon 6e / Part-Dieu / Brotteaux looking for a hairdresser: mostly women (cut, colour, highlights, Brazilian straightening, wedding styling), also men and children. They arrive from Google, Planity or word of mouth, often on a phone, and want to know quickly: who is Simon, what does it cost, when is it open, and how to book.

## Product Purpose

Showcase site for an independent hair salon run by one person, Simon Bouvatier. Success = visitors trust Simon enough to book (via Planity) or call for services not bookable online (lissage, mariage, extensions).

## Positioning

A one-man salon: the client always deals with Simon himself — visagiste, styliste, coloriste. Not a chain, not a team rota. Clients describe him as warm, attentive, taking his time ("à l'écoute il a pris son temps pour réaliser la couleur que je souhaitais depuis longtemps").

## Operating Context

- Address: 75 rue Pierre Corneille, 69006 Lyon — between métro Cordeliers and Part-Dieu.
- Hours: mardi–samedi 9h–19h; closed Sunday and Monday.
- Booking: Planity (https://www.planity.com/simon-bouvatier-69006-lyon), 24h/24, payment on site, instant confirmation. Lissage, defrisage, mariage, chignon, extensions and barbe are not bookable online → call.
- Price level: €€.

## Product Principles

1. Simon is the product: one name, one pair of hands — show the person, not a brand facade.
2. Prices visible and honest, exactly as on Planity.
3. Booking in one tap from anywhere on the page; phone for non-bookable services.
4. Practical info (address, métro, hours) never more than a scroll away.
